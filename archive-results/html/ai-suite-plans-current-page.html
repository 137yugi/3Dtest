The AI Suite plans explained

13 min read

The AI Suite is Artlist’s all-in-one solution that combines powerful AI tools to streamline your creative workflow. With AI Suite you can generate AI voiceovers, images and videos using a variety of language and styles. From smart search to automated asset curation, this plan helps you find, organize, and use content faster and more efficiently, so you can focus on creating. This article will summarize what the plan includes and what license it comes with.

What license is included?

The AI Suite plans come with a Pro license. This allows you to use AI generated assets in your video projects on any platform worldwide including social media, TV, films, streaming, and broadcast, including ads, commercial and client work.

What plans are available?

There are three AI Suite plans. The AI Starter plan, AI Creator Plan, and AI Professional plan. All plans are available yearly or monthly and come with credits that allow you to access Artlist Studio and create voiceovers, images, videos, and music on Artlist’s Toolkit.

Each subscription plan includes a fixed number of credits, and fast and parallel generations per month(tier dependent), which reset each month on the date of credit renewal. Unused credits and fast generations do not carry over into the next month's renewal.  Whenever you generate AI content - whether it’s voiceover, image, video, or music, credits are deducted based on the specific tool and settings used. Learn more.

How Unlimited Generations Work

Unlimited gives you the freedom to create with selected models without worrying about credits. It’s designed to help you stay in your creative flow and iterate freely subject to reasonable use, daily usage thresholds per account, and the restrictions described below.

  • No credit deductions: Generate on supported models without deducting from your credit balance.
  • Continuous access: Keep creating on supported models even after your credits run out, unless reasonable use limits, daily usage thresholds, which may vary by selected AI model, service-performance protections, or misuse restrictions apply. 

How Fast Generations Work

Every month, you get a fixed number of top-priority fast generations, depending on your plan. Once your fast generations run out, you still have unlimited access, generations simply process at standard speed.

How Parallel Generations Work

Parallel generations determine how many image and video assets a single user can create at the same time in Unlimited mode. Your plan includes a set limit (e.g., 8, 10, or 12) on how many assets you can generate at once. Once you reach the maximum number of parallel generations, you will be unable to generate until another asset finishes loading.

When you upgrade:
 
  • The number of credits in the package are the total that you will receive per month.
  • For example, if you upgrade from the 40,000 credit package to 240,000 credit package your total credit amount per month will be 240,000.
  • Credits and fast generations don’t roll over. Used credits are deducted from the total amount.
  • For example, if you used 1,000 credits before your upgrade to 240,000 monthly credits, you will receive 239,000 credits out of 240,000 credits.

If you upgrade from a monthly plan to a higher-tier monthly plan, or from a yearly plan to a higher-tier yearly plan, your renewal date stays the same as your original purchase date.

If you upgrade from a monthly plan to a yearly plan, the yearly plan starts immediately, and your renewal date resets to the upgrade date.

Note: For all upgrades, you pay the full price minus your balance from your current plan.  On your next renewal you’ll be charged the standard rate for the plan you upgraded too.

Commonly asked questions

How do I upgrade to get more credits?

To upgrade your credits:

  1. Log in to your Artlist account.
  2. Navigate to My Account under your Profile settings.
  3. Under Overview, click Upgrade to get more credits.
  4. Select the credit amount you want.
  5. Purchase, and the credits will be upgraded immediately.

 

What happens to my credits when I upgrade?

When you upgrade, your monthly credit limit changes to match the new plan. Your new plan’s total credit limit applies right away. Any credits you already used in the current billing cycle are deducted from that new limit (it doesn’t “stack” a fresh full bundle on top).  For example, if you used 1,000 credits before your upgrade to 240,000 monthly credits, you will receive 239,000 credits out of 240,000 credits. Your full credit limit refreshes on your next renewal date. You’ll be charged a prorated amount for the rest of the current cycle, then the full new plan price at the next renewal. 

I am on the 500,000 credit plan and want to upgrade to 5 million credits, but I am not seeing the 5 million credit option in the upgrade. Why not?

When upgrading, you can only see and select from the next 3 tiers above your current plan.
Since you’re on the 500,000-credit plan, the available upgrade options are the 1 million, 1.5 million, and 2 million.

To reach the 5 million credit plan, you’ll first need to upgrade to the 2 million credit plan. Once that upgrade is complete, the 5 million credit option will become available.

Can I purchase additional AI credits as a one off?

Generally, you are unable to purchase additional AI credits as a one-off. To increase your limit, you can upgrade to a higher credit package, which will adjust your monthly or yearly plan and apply the new credit allowance moving forward.

Exception for High-Tier Plans: If you are currently on the AI Suite 80,000  plan or above, you do have the option to purchase one-off top-up credits as needed and usable immediately on successful payment.

Note: Unlike your standard plan allowance, these one-off top-up credits will expire 30 days after the date of purchase.

Where can I see my renewal cost after an upgrade?

On your next renewal you’ll be charged the standard rate for the plan you upgraded too. When upgrading, you will see the renewal cost in the disclaimer on the checkout page. 

Where can I find out more information on AI voiceover?

I am interested in the AI Creator monthly plan. Will I have access to Unlimited?

No. To access unlimited you need to have an AI Creator yearly plan.

Do unused credits and fast generations roll over to the next month?

AI credits and fast generations do not roll over from month to month. Monthly credits reset at the beginning of each monthly credit cycle.

Is there a benefit to choosing the yearly plan over monthly?

The benefit of choosing a yearly plan over a monthly plan is overall cost. The yearly plan will be more cost efficient in the long run.
